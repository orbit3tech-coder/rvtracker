FS/RV Tracker — Data and Privacy Policy

Effective 10 November 2026 · Orbit Technologies

FS/RV Tracker, made by Orbit Technologies, keeps your records on your phone. We have no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. Our server receives data only when you use the optional group features (FS group updates and the group map), and the names and phone numbers of the people you visit are never sent to it. The app also asks OpenStreetMap and your phone's own services for place names, addresses, map tiles and speech typing, as described below.

This policy covers the FS/RV Tracker Android app, version 4.7 and earlier versions. It takes effect on 10 November 2026.

Location

FS/RV Tracker reads your precise location only to mark where an entry was made. The app reads it once when you open a new entry, and again when you tap to capture the location. FS meeting entries do not capture it automatically.

  • Never in the background: the app does not track your movements, record routes or ask for "all the time" location.
  • Place name and address: after a location is captured, the app asks your phone's Geocoder and OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service for the place name and the full address, which may include a house or building number. It fills only empty fields and never overwrites what you typed. The address refresh button replaces the address only after you confirm.
  • Offline: with no internet, nothing is looked up and the fields stay empty.
  • Moving the pin or searching the map: the place you type in the map search box goes to Nominatim to find it.

Your location is sent to Orbit Technologies only as part of a group map upload or an FS group update that you choose to make (see Group features).

Your records, voice clips and backups

Your tags and visits are kept in the app's private storage on your phone (visits.json), with voice clips in the same place. Hours, credit hours, day notes and settings are kept in the app's private settings. When the app is updated, it first makes a safety copy of your records on the phone.

  • Android backup: if Backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the app's data, including your records, to your Google account under Google's terms. You can turn this off in Settings → Google → Backup.
  • Files you save or share: backups, exports and tag files (.rvt.json) contain the tags you choose, including names and phone numbers. Tag files also hold future reminders. KML map exports and voice exports are files too. They go only where you choose to save or send them. Once you share them, the person or service you share them with controls them, so keep backups somewhere private.
  • Share as text: the tag's details go to the app you pick, such as WhatsApp or e-mail. Reminders are not included.
  • Importing or restoring: the app reads only the file you open and adds its tags on your phone. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Calling, messaging and maps: the app opens your phone, SMS, WhatsApp, maps or calendar app with the details filled in. It does not read anything back from them.

Group features

Both group features are optional and need a four-letter group code. Our group server runs on Cloudflare and stores only what is listed here.

  • FS group update, captain: when the captain publishes an FS tag, the app sends the group code, the tag name, the FS date and time, the meeting date and time, the place and its location, the meeting details, the captain's name and the reminder times still to come. The FS contact number is never sent.
  • FS group update, members: when a member opens or checks an FS tag in a group, the app sends the group code and the tag name, and receives the captain's latest update.
  • Group map upload: only when you tap Upload and confirm, the app sends the group code and, for each entry, the tag, the place, the address, the location, the visit time and the Do not Call mark. No names or phone numbers are uploaded. The app shows this before every upload, so you can edit or clear an address first. Uploading again after an edit updates the pin.
  • Captain actions: creating, downloading, deleting pins from or clearing a group map sends the group code and the captain's PIN.

Anyone who has the group code can receive that group's FS updates, and the captain can download its map. Share a group code only with people in your group.

Services the app connects to

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in, and we don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • No advertising, and no advertising or tracking IDs.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools of our own.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, call log or SMS.
  • No background location tracking.
  • We never upload the names or phone numbers of the people you visit, your voice clips, your hours or your notes.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your data.

Keeping and deleting your data

Everything the app stores stays on your phone until you delete it, and you can delete it at any time.

  • One entry or tag: open it and use Delete.
  • Everything on the phone: Settings → Apps → FS/RV Tracker → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Copies in your Google account backup follow Google's backup settings.
  • Group map pins: the group captain can delete pins or clear the whole map in the app.
  • FS group updates: only the captain's latest update is kept; each new one replaces it.
  • Anything on our server: email us with the group code and we will delete that group's data.
  • Files you saved or shared stay wherever you saved or sent them until you delete them there.

Children

FS/RV Tracker is a ministry record keeper for general audiences and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18.

Security

Records, voice clips and settings are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. All connections the app makes use encrypted HTTPS. Captain actions on a group map need the captain's PIN. Anyone who can unlock your phone can see your records, so keep a screen lock on.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to know what personal data is processed about you, to have it corrected or erased, to nominate someone to act for you, and to have grievances addressed. Most of your data never leaves your phone, so you can see, correct and delete it yourself in the app.

  • Turn features off at any time: location and microphone in your phone's settings; group features by not adding a group code.
  • People in your records: the names, numbers and addresses you record are personal data of other people. Keep them only as long as you need them, and delete them when asked.
  • Data held by OpenStreetMap or Google: requests about their logs go to them, under their privacy policies.
  • Anything else: contact us using the details below. You may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently, we will update this policy, change the date at the top, and point out important changes in the app's release notes before they take effect.
